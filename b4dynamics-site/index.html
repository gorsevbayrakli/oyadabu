01 Chapter one — The problem
Monday, 7:40 AMAt a pharmaceutical manufacturer, an ordinary start to the week.

Production manager Maria opens her laptop. Before her coffee cools, the screen fills with notifications. Stock figures don't match the shop floor, finance is working from a different spreadsheet, and quality assurance is hunting for evidence ahead of the audit.

The company has grown, but its systems haven't kept up. Every department has its own version of the truth. Maria's job is to run production, yet half her day goes into chasing data.

* Maria is a composite character based on situations our customers often describe.

03 Chapter three — The plan

Instead of chaos, four clear steps.

Maria didn't need more software. She needed a roadmap she could trust. We follow the same transparent approach on every project.

1

Understand

We map your processes in workshops, on the shop floor and with your teams. The “why” comes before the “how”.

2
